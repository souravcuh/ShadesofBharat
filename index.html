HOW WE TELL THE STORY

Tradition with Context

Devotional traditions are presented with respect. Scriptural references, Rishi-parampara, inscriptions, architecture, archaeology and later scholarship are identified according to their own evidentiary character rather than blended into a single claim.

Śruti & SmṛtiVeda, Upanishadic, Itihasa and related traditions where applicable.
Purāṇa & ĀgamaTextual traditions relevant to temples, tirthas, deities and ritual life.
Rishi ParamparāTeachings, lineages and living intellectual traditions.
Documented HistoryInscriptions, archaeology, architecture and carefully sourced scholarship.
