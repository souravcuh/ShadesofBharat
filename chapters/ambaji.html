अंबाजी

Ambaji

Near the Gujarat–Rajasthan border — tradition holds Sati's heart fell here. The sanctum, like a handful of other Shakti Peethas, holds no idol at all.

बनासकांठा, गुजरात · Banaskantha, Gujarat  —  देवी (अंबा माँ) · Devi (Amba Mata)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Ambaji is counted among the sites where a part of Sati's body — here, the heart — is said to have fallen, and is revered as the kuldevi (family deity) of many communities across Gujarat and Rajasthan.

मंदिर

The Temple

As at Jwalamukhi, worship here centers not on an image but on a Vishwa Yantra, a geometric diagram, kept veiled in the sanctum. The temple's golden-domed shikhara, rebuilt in the 20th century, is visible for miles across the surrounding plain.
