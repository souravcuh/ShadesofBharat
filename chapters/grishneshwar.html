घृष्णेश्वर

Grishneshwar

Traditionally counted as the twelfth and last of the Jyotirlingas, just beside the Ellora cave temples cut into the same hillside.

औरंगाबाद, महाराष्ट्र · Aurangabad, Maharashtra  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition tells of a devotee, Kusuma, whose unwavering faith and grief over her son restored him to life by Shiva's grace at this spot — the temple's older name, Ghushmeshwar, is drawn from her devotion.

मंदिर

The Temple

Rebuilt in red basalt in the 18th century by Ahilyabai Holkar — the same patron behind the present Kashi Vishwanath temple — Grishneshwar stands a short distance from the rock-cut Ellora caves, placing a living Jyotirlinga and a UNESCO World Heritage monument within walking distance of each other.
