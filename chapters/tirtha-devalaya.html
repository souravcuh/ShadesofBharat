तीर्थ एवं देवालय

Tirtha & Devalaya

A tirtha is literally a "crossing place" — a point where the sacred is close enough to reach. Across the subcontinent these crossings cluster into a few great circuits: the Char Dham, the Jyotirlingas, the Shakti Peethas, the Sangams, and the temples of the great avatars. Tap any card below for the fuller story.

चतुर्दिशाओं का तीर्थ

The Char Dham — Pilgrimage of the Four Directions

Tradition credits the 8th-century philosopher Adi Shankaracharya with founding four abodes at the far corners of the subcontinent — one each in the north, south, east, and west — so that a single pilgrimage would carry a devotee across the whole of Bharat.

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand

Vishnu as Badrinarayan, seated on the Alaknanda's bank between the Nar and Narayan ranges — the northern anchor of the pan-India Char Dham, and the closing stop of the Himalayan circuit.

हिमालय की चार धाम यात्रा

The Himalayan Char Dham — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath

A separate, smaller circuit set entirely in Uttarakhand's high Himalaya — open only for the months between snowmelt and the next winter's close. Not to be confused with the pan-India Char Dham above, though Badrinath and Kedarnath each belong to a second circuit too.

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand

The source shrine of the Yamuna, set beside a glacier at over 3,200 m — the westernmost and usually the first stop on the Himalayan Char Dham.

द्वादश ज्योतिर्लिंग

The Twelve Jyotirlingas

A jyotirlinga is a "linga of light" — a site where Shiva is held to have appeared as a self-manifest column of radiance rather than an image installed by human hands. All twelve traditionally named sites follow.

सोमनाथ
Somnath
गुजरात · Gujarat

Reckoned first among the twelve Jyotirlingas, on the Arabian Sea coast. Destroyed and rebuilt repeatedly across the centuries, most recently after 1951.

शक्ति पीठ

The Shakti Peethas

Legend holds that when Shiva carried Sati's body in grief, Vishnu's discus cut it apart, and each place a piece fell became a Shakti Peetha — a site where the Devi herself, not an image of her, is held to reside. Traditional counts range from 18 to 51 (some say 108); twelve widely revered ones follow.

कामाख्या
Kamakhya
गुवाहाटी, असम · Guwahati, Assam

On Nilachal Hill above the Brahmaputra — one of the most important Shakti Peethas, marking the spot tradition holds as Sati's womb.

नदियों का संगम

Sangam — Where the Rivers Meet

A sangam is a river confluence, and a handful carry particular weight in tradition: Prayagraj's Triveni, where the Ganga and Yamuna meet a third, invisible Saraswati, and the Panch Prayag — five confluences upstream in Uttarakhand where the Alaknanda gathers the tributaries that make it, by Devprayag, the Ganga itself.

त्रिवेणी संगम, प्रयागराज
Triveni Sangam, Prayagraj
उत्तर प्रदेश · Uttar Pradesh

Where the Ganga and Yamuna meet the Saraswati — held in tradition to flow here unseen — making this the most sacred of all river confluences and host to the Kumbh Mela.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
विष्णुप्रयाग
Vishnuprayag
उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand

The first and highest of the Panch Prayag — where the Alaknanda meets the Dhauliganga, close to the route toward Badrinath and the Valley of Flowers.

Nandprayag
उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand

Where the Alaknanda meets the Nandakini — tradition connects the town to King Nanda, said to have performed a great yajna here.

Karnaprayag
उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand

Where the Alaknanda meets the Mandakini, the river that flows down from Kedarnath — a junction on the route to both Kedarnath and Badrinath.

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand

The last and most sacred of the Panch Prayag — where the Alaknanda meets the Bhagirathi, and the river takes the name Ganga for the first time.

अवतार एवं देव मंदिर

Avatar & Deity Mandirs

Beyond the great circuits, a handful of individual temples are so closely tied to a single deity that they anchor that deity's worship across the whole tradition — Hanuman, Krishna, Ganesha, Saraswati, and Lakshmi among them.

हनुमानगढ़ी, अयोध्या
Hanuman Garhi, Ayodhya
उत्तर प्रदेश · Uttar Pradesh

A fortress-like hilltop shrine in Ayodhya, traditionally the first stop for pilgrims before visiting Rama's birthplace nearby.

Banke Bihari, Vrindavan
उत्तर प्रदेश · Uttar Pradesh

In Vrindavan, the town of Krishna's childhood — Krishna worshipped here in his youthful, flute-playing form, "Banke Bihari".

One of Mumbai's most visited temples — Ganesha worshipped here as Siddhivinayak, "the one who grants siddhi (accomplishment)".

महाराष्ट्र · Maharashtra

Overlooking the Arabian Sea — one of Mumbai's oldest and most visited temples to Lakshmi, goddess of wealth and prosperity.
