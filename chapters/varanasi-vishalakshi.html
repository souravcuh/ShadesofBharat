विशालाक्षी, वाराणसी

Vishalakshi, Varanasi

Near Manikarnika Ghat in Varanasi — "the wide-eyed one," a second great Shakti site in the same city that holds the Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga.

उत्तर प्रदेश · Uttar Pradesh  —  देवी (विशालाक्षी) · Devi (Vishalakshi)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition holds this as the spot where an earring of Sati fell, and the goddess is worshipped here as Vishalakshi, "she of the wide eyes" — placing one of the eighteen Maha Shakti Peethas in the same city already sacred to Shiva as Vishwanath.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple sits in the narrow lanes near Manikarnika Ghat, one of Varanasi's principal cremation ghats on the Ganga, within easy walking distance of Kashi Vishwanath — making the two temples together a common single pilgrimage circuit through the old city.
