जन्माष्टमी

Janmashtami

The midnight birth of Krishna in a prison cell in Mathura, to Devaki and Vasudeva, under a sky the legend says was thrown into darkness and rain so his father could carry him to safety across the Yamuna. Marked with fasting until midnight, cradle ceremonies, and, in Maharashtra, the dahi handi — human towers built to break a pot of curd hung out of reach, re-enacting young Krishna's mischief.

भाद्रपद कृष्ण अष्टमी

कथा एवं उत्पत्ति

Story & Origins

Vrindavan and Mathura, Krishna's childhood homes, host the year's largest celebrations, with Ras Lila performances re-enacting his youth staged nightly in the lead-up to the festival.

कैसे मनाया जाता है

How It's Kept

ISKCON temples worldwide hold some of the largest Janmashtami gatherings outside India itself, a reflection of the festival's global reach through the Krishna consciousness movement.
