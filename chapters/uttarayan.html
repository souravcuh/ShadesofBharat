उत्तरायण

Uttarayan

Gujarat's kite-flying festival, marking the same solar transition. Rooftops fill from dawn as thousands of kites compete across the sky, cotton-thread lines coated in glass powder for cutting rival kites.

गुजरात

कथा एवं उत्पत्ति

Story & Origins

Ahmedabad hosts an International Kite Festival timed to Uttarayan, drawing kite-flyers from dozens of countries to compete and exhibit alongside the local celebration.

कैसे मनाया जाता है

How It's Kept

Rooftop gatherings run from sunrise past sunset, with festival foods like undhiyu (a mixed-vegetable dish) and til-laddoo eaten between kite duels.
