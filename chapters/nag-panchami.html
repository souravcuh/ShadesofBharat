नाग पंचमी

Nag Panchami

A day of reverence for serpents (nagas), honored as guardians of water, fertility, and the underworld — offerings of milk are made at snake shrines and anthills, and Shiva is worshipped with the serpent Vasuki coiled at his neck in mind.

श्रावण शुक्ल पंचमी

कथा एवं उत्पत्ति

Story & Origins

The day is linked in some tellings to Krishna subduing the serpent Kaliya in the Yamuna — taming rather than killing him, and banishing him instead, a story of restraint over destruction.

कैसे मनाया जाता है

How It's Kept

Live snakes were traditionally brought by snake charmers for ritual worship in some regions, a practice increasingly replaced today by symbolic clay or drawn images amid wildlife conservation concerns.
