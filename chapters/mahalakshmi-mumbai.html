महालक्ष्मी मंदिर, मुंबई

Mahalakshmi Temple, Mumbai

Overlooking the Arabian Sea — one of Mumbai's oldest and most visited temples to Lakshmi, goddess of wealth and prosperity.

महाराष्ट्र · Maharashtra  —  महालक्ष्मी · Mahalakshmi

Avatar Mandir
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Local tradition holds that a British engineer, struggling to complete a sea-facing causeway nearby in the 18th century, was guided in a dream to recover images of the goddess from the sea, and built this temple after the causeway's successful completion.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple houses three deities together — Mahakali, Mahalakshmi, and Mahasaraswati — set into a rock-cut sanctum, with the sea visible just beyond. It remains one of the most visited temples in the city, particularly during Navratri.
