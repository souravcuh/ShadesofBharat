Śākta Pīṭhasशक्ति पीठ

This page presents the 51-Pīṭha tradition of the Pīṭhanirṇaya / Mahāpīṭhanirūpaṇa. Each entry keeps the textual Pīṭha name, associated aṅga, Devī and Bhairava editable as text. Living regional identifications are respected, and places where manuscripts or traditions differ are marked rather than forced into a single claim.

Why 51 here?

The Śākta Pīṭha tradition is transmitted in more than one enumeration. The Pīṭhanirṇaya, also known as the Mahāpīṭhanirūpaṇa, is a major textual tradition that gives fifty-one Pīṭhas together with the associated Devī, Bhairava and aṅga. D. C. Sircar prepared a critical edition from multiple manuscripts and related textual materials.

Devotional respect: a “variant” label does not mean a temple or regional belief is being rejected. It means that more than one honoured textual or living tradition survives, and Shades of Bharat will present those traditions side by side.

Other sacred enumerations

The Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Book 7, Chapter 30, preserves a wider enumeration of 108 Pīṭhas. Other Śākta, Purāṇic and regional traditions use additional counts. Therefore this page should be read as the complete 51-Pīṭha register of the selected textual tradition, not as a declaration that other sacred enumerations are invalid.

Detailed temple histories, photographs, local sthala-purāṇa traditions and architectural notes will be added individually after source verification.
