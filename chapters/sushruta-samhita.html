सुश्रुत संहिता

Sushruta Samhita

The Ayurvedic tradition's foundational surgical text, attributed to Sushruta, cataloguing over a hundred instruments and describing procedures including early reconstructive surgery.

लगभग ६ठी शताब्दी ई.पू. · circa 6th century BCE

Ayurvedic Text
विषय-वस्तु व संरचना

Content & Structure

Organized across six sthanas, the Samhita covers surgical training methods, including practice incisions on vegetables and dead animals before live procedures, over 300 surgical procedures, and detailed pre- and post-operative care.

प्रभाव व महत्त्व

Influence & Significance

Its description of a forehead-flap technique for nasal reconstruction was studied by 18th-century European surgeons after colonial-era encounters with Indian practitioners using it, feeding directly into the early development of modern plastic surgery.
