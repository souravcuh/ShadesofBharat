खान-पान

Khan-Pan — Food

What is eaten, how it is classified, and how a meal itself is put together — food as a daily practice shaped by centuries of thought on balance and purity.

सात्विक आहार
Satvik Ahar
खान-पान · Khan-Pan

The Ayurvedic classification of food by its effect on the mind and body — satvik (pure, calming), rajasik (stimulating), and tamasik (heavy, dulling) — a framework that shapes what many households choose to cook and avoid.

थाली
Thali
खान-पान · Khan-Pan

The traditional plate-format meal — a central serving of rice or bread surrounded by small bowls (katori) of vegetables, lentils, yogurt, and sweet, meant to balance every taste in a single sitting.

प्रसाद
Prasad
खान-पान · Khan-Pan

Food first offered to a deity and then distributed to devotees — turning a meal into a shared act of grace rather than a private one.

व्रत का भोजन
Vrat Bhojan
खान-पान · Khan-Pan

A distinct style of fasting food eaten on vrat, observance days — built around grains and spices considered permissible during a fast, rather than an absence of food altogether.

क्षेत्रीय व्यंजन
Kshetriya Vyanjan (Regional Cuisine)
खान-पान · Khan-Pan

A subcontinent's worth of distinct culinary traditions, each shaped by local climate, crops, and history, but sharing a common grammar of spice, grain, and ritual food.

रहन-सहन

Rahan-Sahan — Home & Living

How a household is shaped and shared — family structure, the home's own layout, and the small daily rituals that mark a threshold as more than an entrance.

संयुक्त परिवार
Sanyukt Parivar (Joint Family)
रहन-सहन · Rahan-Sahan

Multiple generations of a family, often several married brothers and their families, sharing one household, income, and set of daily responsibilities under a common elder.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
आंगन
Aangan (Courtyard Home)
रहन-सहन · Rahan-Sahan

The open central courtyard at the heart of traditional home design — a shared, sky-open space for daily chores, family gathering, and ritual alike.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
तुलसी का पौधा
Tulsi Paudha (The Sacred Tulsi Plant)
रहन-सहन · Rahan-Sahan

The holy basil plant kept in the courtyard or doorway of countless Hindu homes, watered and circled each morning as a living, daily act of devotion.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
रंगोली
Rangoli
रहन-सहन · Rahan-Sahan

Geometric patterns drawn fresh each day, or for a festival, at a home's threshold using colored powder, rice flour, or flower petals — a welcome mat remade by hand.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
अतिथि देवो भव
Atithi Devo Bhava
रहन-सहन · Rahan-Sahan

'The guest is a god' — a Sanskrit precept placing hospitality toward a guest, expected or not, on the same footing as devotion to the divine.

चलन-बलन

Chalan-Balan — Custom & Conduct

The everyday gestures and life-stage rites that carry respect, blessing, and belonging between people.

नमस्ते
Namaste
चलन-बलन · Chalan-Balan

The palms-together greeting gesture, held before the chest with a slight bow — 'I bow to the divine in you,' offered to strangers and family alike.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
चरण स्पर्श
Charan Sparsh
चलन-बलन · Chalan-Balan

Touching an elder's feet as a gesture of respect, typically returned with a spoken blessing — a small daily ritual of the guru-shishya relationship extended into family life.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
सोलह संस्कार
Solah Sanskar (The Sixteen Life-Cycle Rites)
चलन-बलन · Chalan-Balan

A traditional sequence of sixteen sacraments marking a person's life from before birth to after death, each meant to refine and prepare the individual for the next stage.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
तिलक
Tilak
चलन-बलन · Chalan-Balan

A mark applied to the forehead in sandalwood paste, turmeric, kumkum, or ash — worn after worship, at festivals, or as an ongoing sign of sect and devotion.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
धारण

Dharan — Dress & Adornment

What is worn, and what it signals — garments and marks carried from one generation to the next with meaning still attached.

साड़ी
Saree
धारण · Dharan

A single length of unstitched cloth, typically five to nine yards, draped rather than sewn — among the oldest and most widely recognized garments still worn daily across the subcontinent.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
धोती
Dhoti
धारण · Dharan

A single length of unstitched cloth wrapped and tucked around the waist and legs — traditional men's dress worn from ordinary days to the most formal religious rituals.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
बिंदी व सिंदूर
Bindi & Sindoor
धारण · Dharan

The forehead dot and the red pigment traditionally worn along a woman's hair-parting — two related but distinct marks, one devotional, the other historically signaling marital status.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
मंगलसूत्र
Mangalsutra
धारण · Dharan

A black-and-gold beaded necklace tied around a bride's neck during the wedding ceremony — literally 'the auspicious thread,' worn afterward as an ongoing sign of marriage.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
पगड़ी
Pagdi (Turban)
धारण · Dharan

A length of cloth wound into a headdress, worn across many regions and communities with styles, colors, and tying methods that each carry their own local meaning.
