द्वारका

Dwarka

Krishna's coastal capital on the Arabian Sea. The Dwarkadhish temple, its spire visible for miles, marks the western point of the Char Dham.

गुजरात · Gujarat  —  कृष्ण · Krishna

Char Dham
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The Mahabharata and Bhagavata Purana describe Dwarka as the city Krishna built and ruled after leaving Mathura — a planned capital reclaimed from the sea, said to have sunk beneath the waves after his passing. Marine archaeological surveys off the modern coast have found submerged structures, though their exact age and connection to the legend remain debated among scholars.

मंदिर

The Temple

The present Dwarkadhish temple, dedicated to Krishna as "king of Dwarka," rises five storeys on 72 pillars and is believed to have origins going back over two thousand years, rebuilt and expanded many times since. It stands near the confluence of the Gomti river and the sea, a short distance from the separate Nageshwar Jyotirlinga.
