चामुंडेश्वरी, मैसूर

Chamundeshwari, Mysore

Atop Chamundi Hill overlooking Mysore — the goddess who gives the city its name, and the traditional slayer of the buffalo-demon Mahishasura.

कर्नाटक · Karnataka  —  देवी (चामुंडा) · Devi (Chamunda)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Chamundeshwari is identified with Durga in her form as slayer of Mahishasura, the buffalo-demon for whom nearby Mysuru (Mahishasura's town) is traditionally named; the goddess was also the tutelary deity of the Mysore royal family for centuries.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple crowns Chamundi Hill, reached by a long stone stairway of over a thousand steps or by road, with a large Nandi statue carved partway up. A monumental statue of the demon Mahishasura, wielding a sword and cobra, stands near the hill's summit.
