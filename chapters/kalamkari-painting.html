कलमकारी

Kalamkari Painting

Hand-drawn or block-printed narrative cloth painting from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, its name meaning 'pen-work' for the tamarind-twig pen traditionally used to draw it.

आंध्र प्रदेश, तेलंगाना · Andhra Pradesh, Telangana

Folk Art
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Two distinct kalamkari traditions developed side by side: Srikalahasti's freehand style, drawn directly onto cloth with a sharpened tamarind twig pen dipped in natural dye, depicting mythological scenes traditionally sold near temples, and Machilipatnam's block-printed style, using carved wooden blocks to stamp repeating patterns, developed for wider trade.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Both towns retain Geographical Indication recognition for their respective kalamkari styles, and the craft's all-natural dyeing process, mordants and dyes drawn from plants, minerals, and even cow dung and buffalo milk in traditional preparation, remains largely unchanged from its centuries-old process today.
