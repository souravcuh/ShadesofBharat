हिमाचली टोपी

Himachali Topi

A flat, rounded woolen cap worn across Himachal Pradesh, its color and trim traditionally signaling the wearer's home valley.

हिमाचल प्रदेश · Himachal Pradesh

Caps
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Woven from local wool and finished with a colored band, often green or maroon, sometimes decorated with a small flower or brooch, the Himachali topi's exact style varies by district, with Kullu's cap distinct from Kinnaur's or Chamba's to a trained local eye.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

The cap remains standard formal and ceremonial wear across the state, presented as a mark of honor to guests and dignitaries much as the pagdi already covered in this site's Jeevan Shaili chapter is elsewhere in the country.
