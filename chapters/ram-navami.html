राम नवमी

Ram Navami

The ninth and closing day of Chaitra Navratri, marking the birth of Rama — prince of Ayodhya and hero of the Ramayana — to King Dasharatha and Queen Kausalya at noon. Temples hold readings of the Ramcharitmanas and a cradle ceremony for the infant Rama.

चैत्र शुक्ल नवमी

कथा एवं उत्पत्ति

Story & Origins

Ayodhya's Ram Janmabhoomi temple holds its largest annual gathering on this day, with the exact traditional moment of Rama's birth marked by a special noon aarti.

कैसे मनाया जाता है

How It's Kept

Many households keep a day-long fast broken only after the noon ritual. Recitations of the Ramcharitmanas or Valmiki Ramayana often run for days beforehand, culminating on Navami itself.
