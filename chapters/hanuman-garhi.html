हनुमानगढ़ी, अयोध्या

Hanuman Garhi, Ayodhya

A fortress-like hilltop shrine in Ayodhya, traditionally the first stop for pilgrims before visiting Rama's birthplace nearby.

उत्तर प्रदेश · Uttar Pradesh  —  हनुमान · Hanuman

Avatar Mandir
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition holds that Hanuman resided here in a cave, guarding the Ayodhya of Rama much as he is said to still guard the town today — pilgrims customarily visit Hanuman Garhi before any other Ayodhya shrine, seeking his permission, as it were, to proceed.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple sits atop a small hill reached by a flight of 76 steps, its fort-like outer walls giving it its name (garhi, "small fort"). It remains one of the most visited shrines in Ayodhya alongside the Ram Janmabhoomi temple.
