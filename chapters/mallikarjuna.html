मल्लिकार्जुन

Mallikarjuna

At Srisailam on the Krishna river — a rare site where a Jyotirlinga (Mallikarjuna, Shiva) and a Shakti Peetha (Bhramaramba, Devi) share the same temple complex.

आंध्र प्रदेश · Andhra Pradesh  —  शिव व पार्वती (भ्रमराम्बा) · Shiva & Parvati (Bhramaramba)

JyotirlingaShakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition holds that Parvati came here as a black bee (bhramara) to reunite with Shiva after their sons Ganesha and Kartikeya married before her — giving the goddess her name, Bhramaramba. The hilltop site, overlooking a gorge on the Krishna river, is also revered as one of the eighteen Maha Shakti Peethas, marking it as sacred to both Shiva and Devi worship at once.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple complex at Srisailam, fortified with high stone walls, sits above the Krishna river within what is now a tiger reserve. Its combined status as both Jyotirlinga and Shakti Peetha makes it one of the few sites on this page belonging to two separate sacred circuits at once.
