महालक्ष्मी, कोल्हापुर

Mahalakshmi, Kolhapur

Kolhapur's Ambabai temple, one of the six holiest Shakti sites in Maharashtra tradition, dedicated to the goddess as Mahalakshmi.

महाराष्ट्र · Maharashtra  —  देवी (महालक्ष्मी / अंबाबाई) · Devi (Mahalakshmi / Ambabai)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The presiding deity is worshipped as Mahalakshmi, goddess of prosperity, though locally known as Ambabai; the temple is counted among the eighteen Maha Shakti Peethas and is one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in Maharashtra.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple's stone image is carved from black basalt and set so that, on specific days each year, the setting sun's rays fall directly on the deity — a solar alignment built into temples of this era. Its architecture, with an open mandapa and intricately carved pillars, dates substantially to the Chalukya period.
