नालुकेट्टु

Nalukettu

Kerala's traditional courtyard house, its name literally meaning 'four blocks' — four wings enclosing a central open courtyard (nadumuttam) at the home's heart.

केरल · Kerala

House Design
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

A nalukettu's four wings traditionally housed different household functions and family members around the central nadumuttam courtyard, built using laterite stone, wood, and sloped tiled or thatched roofing suited to Kerala's monsoon climate, with the courtyard itself often used for daily rituals and family gatherings.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Larger versions with additional courtyards, ettukettu (eight blocks) or pathinarukettu (sixteen blocks), were built by wealthier landholding families, and while few new nalukettu homes are built today, many surviving examples have been restored as heritage homestays across Kerala.
