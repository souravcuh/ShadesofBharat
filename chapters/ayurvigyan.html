आयुर्विज्ञान

Ayurvigyan (Life & Longevity Science)

Ayurveda's diagnostic and preventive science beyond treatment alone — pulse reading, rejuvenation therapy, and a physiology built on the three doshas that still frames how Ayurvedic practitioners assess a person today.

आयुर्वेद परंपरा · Ayurvedic tradition

Life Science
विषय-वस्तु व संरचना

Diagnosis & Rejuvenation

Nadi Pariksha, pulse diagnosis, reads the rhythm and quality of the pulse at the wrist to assess the balance of vata, pitta, and kapha within a patient, a method still taught and practiced without any instrument beyond the physician's own fingers. Rasayana, the science of rejuvenation, developed detailed protocols using herbs, minerals, and diet aimed at extending healthy life rather than only treating disease.

प्रभाव व महत्त्व

Influence & Significance

This preventive, constitution-first approach, treating each body's balance as individual rather than applying one standard treatment to everyone, remains Ayurveda's most distinctive contribution to medicine, and continues to shape wellness practices worldwide well beyond the clinical treatment of illness.
