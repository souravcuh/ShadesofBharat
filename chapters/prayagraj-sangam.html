त्रिवेणी संगम, प्रयागराज

Triveni Sangam, Prayagraj

Where the Ganga and Yamuna meet the Saraswati — held in tradition to flow here unseen — making this the most sacred of all river confluences and host to the Kumbh Mela.

उत्तर प्रदेश · Uttar Pradesh  —  गंगा · यमुना · सरस्वती · Ganga · Yamuna · Saraswati

Sangam
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The Saraswati is described in later tradition as an underground, invisible river joining the Ganga and Yamuna at this exact point, making the confluence a meeting of three sacred waters rather than two — hence "Triveni," the triple braid.

मंदिर

The Temple

Prayagraj's Sangam hosts the Kumbh Mela on a roughly twelve-year cycle and the smaller Magh Mela annually, drawing among the largest peaceful gatherings of people anywhere on earth. Boats ferry pilgrims to the exact point of confluence, marked by the visibly differing colors of the Ganga and Yamuna's waters.
