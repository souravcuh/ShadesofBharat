नागेश्वर

Nageshwar

A short distance from Dwarka city — Shiva here as "lord of serpents," marked today by a colossal outdoor statue visible from far around.

द्वारका, गुजरात · Dwarka, Gujarat  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The Shiva Purana describes Nageshwar as the linga Shiva manifested to rescue a devotee, Supriya, from a serpent-demon named Daruka — giving the site its name, "lord of serpents (naga)," and its traditional association with protection from poison and fear.

मंदिर

The Temple

The modern temple complex is fronted by a roughly 25-metre seated statue of Shiva, one of the tallest of its kind, making the site visible well before arrival. It lies a short drive from the separate Dwarkadhish temple in Dwarka city.
