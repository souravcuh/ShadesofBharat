राजस्थानी लघु चित्रकला

Rajasthani Miniature Painting

Finely detailed court painting on paper and ivory, worked in distinct regional schools across Rajasthan, each with its own color palette and preferred subject.

राजस्थान · Rajasthan

Folk Art
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Rajasthani miniature painting developed across several royal courts, each forming its own school: Mewar's paintings favor bold color and courtly scenes, Bundi and Kota are known for hunting scenes set in dense forest, and Kishangarh is famous for its elongated-eyed Bani Thani portraits, with religious subjects, Krishna's life among the most common, running through nearly all of them.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Udaipur and Jaipur remain centers where artists continue the technique on paper using fine squirrel-hair brushes and natural pigments, and miniature reproductions of Rajasthani court paintings remain among the state's most recognized art exports today.
