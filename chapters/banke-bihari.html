बांके बिहारी, वृंदावन

Banke Bihari, Vrindavan

In Vrindavan, the town of Krishna's childhood — Krishna worshipped here in his youthful, flute-playing form, "Banke Bihari".

उत्तर प्रदेश · Uttar Pradesh  —  कृष्ण · Krishna

Avatar Mandir
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition holds that the saint Swami Haridas, through his devotion and music, caused Krishna and Radha's combined form to manifest as the image now worshipped here — "banke" (bent in three places) describing the deity's characteristic triple-flexed standing pose.

मंदिर

The Temple

Unlike most temples, the curtain before the deity here is opened and closed every few seconds rather than left open, out of a tradition holding that the deity's gaze itself carries too much power to rest on any one devotee for long. Vrindavan around it holds dozens of other Krishna temples, including Nidhivan and the newer Prem Mandir.
