फुलकारी

Phulkari

'Flower work' — Punjab's signature embroidery, worked from the reverse side of the cloth in long, floss-silk darning stitches that build dense geometric and floral fields.

पंजाब · Punjab

Weaving
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Traditionally embroidered by women on hand-spun cotton (khaddar) using untwisted silk floss thread, phulkari is worked from the cloth's back so the embroiderer builds the pattern without seeing the front directly, with bagh, a denser variant covering the entire cloth surface, considered the style's most elaborate form.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Traditionally made as part of a bride's trousseau, phulkari today carries Geographical Indication recognition and remains widely worn as shawls and dupattas across Punjab, alongside contemporary use on bags and home textiles.
