तुलसी का पौधा

Tulsi Paudha (The Sacred Tulsi Plant)

The holy basil plant kept in the courtyard or doorway of countless Hindu homes, watered and circled each morning as a living, daily act of devotion.

रहन-सहन · Rahan-Sahan

Home & Living
उत्पत्ति व अर्थ

Origins & Meaning

Tulsi is held sacred as an embodiment of the goddess Vrinda, closely associated with Vishnu, and is traditionally grown in a raised, often decorated pot, the tulsi chaura, placed where it receives morning sun, with many households lighting a lamp beside it each evening.

आज के समय में

In Practice Today

Beyond devotion, tulsi is also valued in Ayurveda for its medicinal properties, and Tulsi Vivah, its ceremonial wedding to Vishnu in the form of a stone, shaligram, is marked in Kartik as a distinct household observance closing the traditional wedding season.
