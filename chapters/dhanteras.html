धनतेरस

Dhanteras

Honors Dhanvantari, the physician-deity said to have emerged from the churning of the cosmic ocean bearing the nectar of immortality. A day for buying gold, silver, or new utensils — the purchase itself is considered to invite prosperity into the year ahead.

कार्तिक कृष्ण त्रयोदशी · दिन १

कथा एवं उत्पत्ति

Story & Origins

A second tradition on this day involves lighting a lamp facing south for Yama, god of death, to ward off untimely death in the household — a ritual known as Yama Deepam.

कैसे मनाया जाता है

How It's Kept

Small footprints are drawn from the door inward with rice flour and vermilion, symbolizing Lakshmi's arrival into the home.
