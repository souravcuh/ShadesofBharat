कर्णप्रयाग

Karnaprayag

Where the Alaknanda meets the Pindar river — tradition ties the site to Karna of the Mahabharata, said to have performed austerities here.

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand  —  अलकनंदा · पिंडार · Alaknanda · Pindar

SangamPanch Prayag
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Local tradition holds that Karna, the Mahabharata's tragic warrior, worshipped Surya at this confluence to earn his famed armor and earrings, lending the town his name.

मंदिर

The Temple

A small temple to Karna and Uma-Devi stands near the confluence; the town is a stop along the route linking the Alaknanda valley to Badrinath further upstream.
