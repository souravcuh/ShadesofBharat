ईश उपनिषद्

Isha Upanishad

The shortest of the principal Upanishads, opening the Shukla Yajurveda with a single teaching: that the divine pervades everything, and true renunciation means enjoying the world without grasping at it.

शुक्ल यजुर्वेद के अंतर्गत · within the Shukla Yajurveda

Upanishad
विषय-वस्तु व शिक्षा

Content & Teaching

In just eighteen verses, the Isha Upanishad opens with 'Ishavasyam idam sarvam,' all this is pervaded by the Lord, then argues that one should live a full worldly life through renunciation rather than by rejecting the world outright, desiring nothing that is not freely given.

प्रभाव व महत्त्व

Influence & Significance

Mahatma Gandhi described the opening verse alone as containing everything needed to know if all other scripture were lost, a text whose small size and insistence on living actively in the world rather than withdrawing from it distinguish it from Upanishads that lean more heavily toward renunciation.
