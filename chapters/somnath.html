सोमनाथ

Somnath

Reckoned first among the twelve Jyotirlingas, on the Arabian Sea coast. Destroyed and rebuilt repeatedly across the centuries, most recently after 1951.

गुजरात · Gujarat  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition holds that Soma, the moon god, built the first temple here in gratitude after Shiva lifted a curse placed on him — giving the site its name, "Lord of the Moon." Puranic accounts describe it as one of the very first places Shiva's light is said to have touched the earth.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple was destroyed and rebuilt numerous times across recorded history; the current structure, in Chaulukya style, was reconstructed after 1951 under the initiative of India's first deputy prime minister, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, on the same coastal site as its predecessors.
