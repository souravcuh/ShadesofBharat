जोगुलाम्बा, आलमपुर

Jogulamba, Alampur

At Alampur on the Tungabhadra river, among a cluster of early Chalukyan temples — the goddess here worshipped in a fierce, yogini form.

तेलंगाना · Telangana  —  देवी (जोगुलाम्बा) · Devi (Jogulamba)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Jogulamba — "the yogini goddess" — is counted among the eighteen Maha Shakti Peethas, tradition holding that Sati's upper teeth fell at this spot on the Tungabhadra's bank.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple stands within the Navabrahma group, a cluster of nine early Chalukyan-era stone temples at Alampur dating to roughly the 7th–8th centuries, prized for their architecture as much as for the goddess worshipped within.
