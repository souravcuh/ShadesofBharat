बनारसी बुनाई

Banarasi Weaving

The gold and silver zari-brocade silk weaving of Varanasi — among the most recognized handloom traditions in the country, still worked on pit looms passed through weaving families.

वाराणसी, उत्तर प्रदेश · Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Weaving
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Banarasi weaving works silk thread against zari, fine gold or silver-wrapped thread, in patterns believed to draw on Mughal-era floral and paisley motifs, with a single sari sometimes taking weeks to a few months on the loom depending on the complexity of its brocade.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

The craft is traditionally organized around family workshops in and around Varanasi, already covered in this site's Tirtha & Devalaya chapter, with distinct roles, weaving, dyeing, and zari preparation, often kept within different specialist households across a single finished piece's production.
