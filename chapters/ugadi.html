उगादि

Ugadi

"Yuga-adi" — the start of an age. Families read the year's forecast from the new Panchang and share ugadi pachadi, a dish blending six tastes to mark that the year ahead, like life, carries sweetness, bitterness, and everything between.

आंध्र प्रदेश, तेलंगाना, कर्नाटक

कथा एवं उत्पत्ति

Story & Origins

Tradition holds this the day Brahma began creating the universe after the deluge — the same association given to Gudi Padwa in Maharashtra, showing the shared lunisolar new-year root across the Deccan under different regional names.

कैसे मनाया जाता है

How It's Kept

Homes decorate doorways with mango-leaf torans and draw rangoli at the threshold. Telugu and Kannada households read the Panchanga Shravanam, a public recitation of the year's astrological forecast, often broadcast on regional television.
