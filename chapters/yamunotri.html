यमुनोत्री

Yamunotri

The source shrine of the Yamuna, set beside a glacier at over 3,200 m — the westernmost and usually the first stop on the Himalayan Char Dham.

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand  —  यमुना देवी · Yamuna Devi

Himalayan Char Dham
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Yamuna is worshipped here as the daughter of Surya, the sun god, and sister of Yama, lord of death — tradition holds that bathing in her waters at the source protects against an untimely end. The river itself is said to emerge from the Champasar glacier near Kalindi peak, close by the temple.

मंदिर

The Temple

The shrine sits near the Yamuna's glacial source and is reached by a steep trek from Janki Chatti; a hot spring beside the temple, Surya Kund, is traditionally used to boil rice and potatoes as offerings, taking advantage of its natural heat. Like the rest of the Himalayan circuit, it is open only a few months each year.
