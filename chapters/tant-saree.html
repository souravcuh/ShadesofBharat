तांत साड़ी

Tant Saree

Bengal's everyday handloom cotton saree — light, crisp, and starched, woven with a distinctive wide, decorative border.

पश्चिम बंगाल · West Bengal

Weaving
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Woven from fine cotton on traditional pit looms, a tant saree is prized for being light and breathable in Bengal's humid climate, with weavers concentrated around Shantipur, Dhaniakhali, and Phulia in West Bengal, each town associated with its own signature border pattern and finishing.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Tant remains the saree most widely worn day-to-day across Bengali households, distinct from the region's silk weaves, and the craft continues largely through weaver cooperatives in the same handloom towns that have produced it for generations.
