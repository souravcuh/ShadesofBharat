Pottery

The potter's wheel and hand-built clay tradition — vessels shaped for cooking, storage, and ritual that remain part of daily life and worship alike.

देशव्यापी · Nationwide

शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Worked by the kumhar (potter) community using a foot- or hand-spun wheel, traditional pottery ranges from plain unglazed water pots (matka) that cool water through evaporation, to painted and glazed wares like Delhi's blue pottery, made from a quartz-based dough rather than clay, and the black pottery of Nizamabad and Manipur's Longpi.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Clay vessels remain central to ritual life covered throughout this site — a kalash at nearly every puja, diyas lit by the thousand at Diwali, and images of deities themselves modeled in clay for immersion festivals like Ganesh Chaturthi and Durga Puja.
