कामाख्या

Kamakhya

On Nilachal Hill above the Brahmaputra — one of the most important Shakti Peethas, marking the spot tradition holds as Sati's womb.

गुवाहाटी, असम · Guwahati, Assam  —  देवी (कामाख्या) · Devi (Kamakhya)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Legend holds that when Shiva carried Sati's body across the sky in grief, Vishnu's discus cut it apart to end his wandering; where the womb is said to have fallen became Kamakhya, one of the most revered of all the Shakti Peethas.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple has no idol in the conventional sense — worship centers on a natural rock fissure, kept covered and periodically bathed, representing the goddess's yoni. The annual Ambubachi Mela, marking the goddess's yearly menstruation, is among the largest tantra-linked gatherings in the country.
