त्र्यंबकेश्वर

Trimbakeshwar

Near the source of the Godavari, one of the peninsula's great rivers — the temple's linga is unusually said to carry three faces within it.

नासिक, महाराष्ट्र · Nashik, Maharashtra  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The temple stands close to Brahmagiri hill, traditionally held as the source of the Godavari river, sometimes called the "Ganga of the south." The linga itself is unusual among the twelve for reportedly containing three small faces, representing Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva together.

मंदिर

The Temple

Built in black stone in the late 18th century under the Peshwas, the temple is one of the few Jyotirlinga shrines where non-Hindus have historically been restricted from the inner sanctum. Nearby Kushavarta Kund, a spring-fed tank, is treated as the river's ritual source.
