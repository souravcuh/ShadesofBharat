कश्मीरी पेपर-माशे

Kashmiri Papier-Mâché

Molded paper pulp shaped into boxes, ornaments, and vases, then hand-painted in fine detail — a craft brought to Kashmir centuries ago and now closely tied to the region's identity.

कश्मीर · Kashmir

Handicraft
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Soaked paper pulp is molded into shape, dried, and coated, then painted in successive layers, gold outlining, and fine floral detail, a technique tradition traces to Central Asian craft brought into Kashmir centuries ago and adapted into the region's own distinct style and motif vocabulary.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Srinagar remains the craft's center, and papier-mâché pieces, from small ornaments to large decorative vases, are among Kashmir's most widely recognized handicraft exports, made by artisan families working the full sequence of molding, base coating, and hand-painting.
