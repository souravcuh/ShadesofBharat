रामेश्वरम्

Rameswaram

Tradition holds that Rama worshipped Shiva here before crossing to Lanka. The Ramanathaswamy temple is counted among both the Char Dham and the twelve Jyotirlingas — one site belonging to two circuits.

तमिलनाडु · Tamil Nadu  —  शिव (रामनाथस्वामी) · Shiva (Ramanathaswamy)

Char DhamJyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The Ramayana tradition holds that Rama, before crossing to Lanka to recover Sita, installed and worshipped a Shiva linga here to atone for the killing of Ravana, a Brahmin by birth. The linga Hanuman is said to have attempted to bring from the Himalaya for the same purpose stands in the temple too, second in worship to the one Sita is said to have shaped from sand.

मंदिर

The Temple

Ramanathaswamy temple is famed for the longest corridor of any Hindu temple in India, its pillared passage running along all four sides of the complex. Twenty-two wells within the temple are traditionally bathed in by pilgrims in sequence, each said to carry a different property.
