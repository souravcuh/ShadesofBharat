चरक

Charaka

Compiler of the Charaka Samhita, the foundational text of Ayurvedic internal medicine, still referenced in Ayurvedic study today.

लगभग १–२री शताब्दी · circa 1st-2nd century CE

Ayurveda
जीवन व कथा

Life & Story

Charaka is held to have redacted and expanded an earlier body of medical teaching attributed to the sage Punarvasu Atreya, transmitted through his student Agnivesha, into the systematic text that carries Charaka's own name.

योगदान व विरासत

Contribution & Legacy

The Charaka Samhita covers diagnosis, physiology, and treatment across eight sections, and its emphasis on observing the individual patient's constitution before prescribing treatment remains a defining principle of Ayurvedic practice, distinguishing it from a one-size-fits-all approach to medicine.
