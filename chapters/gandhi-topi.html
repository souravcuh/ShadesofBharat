गांधी टोपी

Gandhi Topi

The simple boat-shaped white cotton cap associated with the independence movement, still worn today as everyday headwear in parts of rural India.

महाराष्ट्र, गुजरात, बिहार · Maharashtra, Gujarat, Bihar

Caps
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Made from plain white khadi, hand-spun cotton cloth, the cap's folded, boat-like shape became widely worn during the independence movement and has remained in use since, particularly among farmers and rural elders in parts of Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Bihar, as ordinary daily headwear rather than ceremonial dress.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

The cap's continued association with khadi ties it to the same hand-spinning and hand-weaving tradition that runs through much of the textile craft covered on this page — cloth made by hand from raw cotton through to a finished, wearable piece.
