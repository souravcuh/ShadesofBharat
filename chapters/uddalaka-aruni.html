उद्दालक आरुणि

Uddalaka Aruni

The Chandogya Upanishad's great teacher, whose lessons to his son Shvetaketu — using a banyan seed and a pinch of salt in water — remain among the tradition's clearest teachings on the nature of the self.

उपनिषद काल · Upanishadic era

Upanishadic Teacher
जीवन व कथा

Life & Story

Uddalaka is described guiding his son Shvetaketu back to a humbler starting point after the young man returned home from years of Vedic study proud of his learning but, his father found, missing its essential point.

योगदान व विरासत

Contribution & Legacy

His teaching culminates in the phrase 'tat tvam asi,' thou art that, repeated across the Chandogya Upanishad's ninth chapter as a direct pointer to the identity of the individual self (atman) with the universal reality (Brahman) — one of the four mahavakyas, great sayings, at the heart of Advaita Vedanta.
