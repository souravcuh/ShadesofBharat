कच्छ भरतकाम

Kutch Embroidery

Densely worked mirror and thread embroidery from the Kutch region, with distinct stitch styles carried by different local communities.

कच्छ, गुजरात · Kutch, Gujarat

Weaving
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Kutch embroidery covers a family of related but distinct styles, Ahir, Rabari, Soof, and others, each associated with a specific pastoral or agricultural community and identifiable by its stitch technique and motif, with small mirrors (abhla) stitched into the pattern a signature shared across most of the region's styles.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Practiced largely by women within the communities that originated each style, Kutch embroidery has grown into a significant livelihood craft through cooperatives formed after the 2001 Gujarat earthquake, connecting village embroiderers directly to wider domestic and export markets.
