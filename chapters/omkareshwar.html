ओंकारेश्वर

Omkareshwar

Set on Mandhata island in the Narmada river, whose shape is traditionally said to resemble the sacred syllable "Om".

मध्य प्रदेश · Madhya Pradesh  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The island in the Narmada on which the temple stands is traditionally described as shaped like the Devanagari symbol for "Om," lending the site its name. A second linga, Amareshwar, stands on the riverbank opposite, and the two are sometimes counted together as a single Jyotirlinga in older listings.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple is reached by a bridge across the Narmada onto Mandhata island, where the linga sits within a multi-tiered shrine. The river itself, one of India's most sacred, is held in some traditions to be even more purifying to look upon than to bathe in.
