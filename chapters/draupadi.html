द्रौपदी

Draupadi

Wife to all five Pandava brothers, born from a sacrificial fire, and the central figure whose public humiliation in the Kaurava court becomes the moral turning point that makes war unavoidable.

महाभारत · Mahabharata

Mahabharata
कथा व भूमिका

Story & Role

Draupadi emerges fully grown from a yajna performed by her father Drupada, and wins herself as Arjuna's bride at her swayamvara archery contest, becoming wife to all five Pandavas after a misunderstanding of their mother Kunti's instruction. When Yudhishthira loses her in a rigged game of dice, she is dragged into the Kaurava court and an attempt is made to disrobe her publicly, an act Krishna is held to have prevented through a miracle of endlessly replenishing cloth.

महत्त्व

Significance

Her demand for justice after that humiliation, and her vow not to bind her hair again until it is washed in Kaurava blood, is treated across the tradition as the moment that makes the Kurukshetra war a moral necessity rather than a political dispute alone, placing Draupadi's grievance at the epic's emotional core.
