बांस गृह

Bamboo House

Lightweight, elevated homes built almost entirely from bamboo — a construction tradition suited to the Northeast's heavy rainfall, humidity, and, in some regions, seismic activity.

पूर्वोत्तर भारत · Northeast India

House Design
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Traditional bamboo houses across Assam, Nagaland, Mizoram, and neighboring states are typically raised on stilts or a bamboo platform, with woven bamboo-mat walls and thatched or tin roofing, a lightweight design that both survives regional earthquakes better than heavier construction and stays cooler and drier in the region's climate.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

Bamboo's fast growth and regional abundance keep the tradition sustainable at a scale few other building materials can match, and contemporary architects working in the Northeast have increasingly returned to bamboo construction techniques for exactly this combination of resilience and low environmental cost.
