महाकालेश्वर

Mahakaleshwar

The only one of the twelve Jyotirlingas facing south. Known for the Bhasma Aarti, performed with sacred ash before sunrise each day.

उज्जैन, मध्य प्रदेश · Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Ujjain's Puranic name, Avantika, marks it as one of the seven cities held to grant liberation (moksha); Shiva is worshipped here as Mahakala, "great time" or "great death," the form said to have subdued a demon that terrorized the city.

मंदिर

The Temple

The linga's south-facing orientation is unique among the twelve and considered tantrically significant. The pre-dawn Bhasma Aarti, in which the deity is bathed in sacred ash, is among the temple's most distinctive and widely known rituals.
