चरक संहिता

Charak Samhita

The foundational text of Ayurvedic internal medicine, compiled by the physician Charaka and still part of Ayurvedic education today.

लगभग १–२री शताब्दी · circa 1st-2nd century CE

Ayurvedic Text
विषय-वस्तु व संरचना

Content & Structure

Structured across eight sthanas (sections) covering diagnosis, anatomy, pharmacology, and treatment, the Samhita organizes disease not just by symptom but by the patient's individual constitution, the doshas of vata, pitta, and kapha, a framework still taught as Ayurveda's diagnostic starting point.

प्रभाव व महत्त्व

Influence & Significance

Alongside the Sushruta Samhita, it forms one of the Brihat Trayi, the 'great three' classical Ayurvedic compendiums, and remains in active clinical reference in Ayurvedic colleges across India today, not merely studied as history. Its author, Charaka, is covered separately in this site's Rishi Parampara chapter.
