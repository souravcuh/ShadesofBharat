गंगोत्री

Gangotri

The temple town for the Ganga, here still called the Bhagirathi. The river's actual glacial source, Gaumukh, lies a trek further upstream.

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand  —  गंगा देवी · Ganga Devi

Himalayan Char Dham
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition holds this as the spot where King Bhagiratha's long penance succeeded in bringing Ganga down from the heavens to purify the ashes of his ancestors — lending the upper river its alternate name, Bhagirathi. A submerged rock in the riverbed near the temple is said to mark where Ganga first touched the earth, said to have been cushioned by Shiva's matted hair.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple itself, built in the 18th century by a Gorkha commander, sits at about 3,100 m; the true glacial source, Gaumukh ("cow's mouth"), lies roughly 18 km further upstream and is reached only on foot.
