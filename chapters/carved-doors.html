Carved Doors

Deeply carved wooden entrance doors, often the most decorated single element of an entire home — the same door-frame motif this site borrows for its own chapter pages.

गुजरात, राजस्थान · Gujarat, Rajasthan

Door Design
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Traditional entrance doors across Gujarat and Rajasthan in particular were carved with floral, geometric, and figurative relief work, sometimes inlaid with brass fittings, and were treated as a household's single most important architectural statement, the first thing a visitor or a deity image passing through the home would encounter.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

The same carving vocabulary appears at vastly different scales, from a modest home's doorway to a haveli's grand entrance already covered on this page, to temple sanctum doors across sites in this site's Tirtha & Devalaya chapter, treating a threshold itself as worthy of the same craft investment as what lies beyond it.
