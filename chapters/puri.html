पुरी

Puri

Home of Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra — wooden deities carved anew every twelve years or so — and of the Rath Yatra, when their chariots are pulled through the streets by the crowd itself.

ओडिशा · Odisha  —  जगन्नाथ (कृष्ण) · Jagannath (Krishna)

Char Dham
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Jagannath — "lord of the universe" — is worshipped here in an unusually simple wooden form alongside his siblings Balabhadra and Subhadra, rather than the more common stone or metal images seen elsewhere. Tradition traces the icons to King Indradyumna, who is said to have commissioned a divine sculptor on the promise the work would never be watched mid-carving — a promise broken, which is why the images remain unfinished-looking to this day.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple's Ratha Yatra, when the three deities travel by giant chariot to the Gundicha temple and back, draws one of the largest annual gatherings anywhere in the world. Puri is also the seat of one of the four cardinal mathas Adi Shankaracharya is credited with founding.
