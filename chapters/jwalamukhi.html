ज्वालामुखी

Jwalamukhi

The goddess worshipped as an eternal flame burning directly from the rock — no idol stands in the sanctum, only the fire itself.

हिमाचल प्रदेश · Himachal Pradesh  —  देवी (ज्वाला जी) · Devi (Jwala Ji)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition holds this as the site where Sati's tongue fell, and the goddess is worshipped here not as an image but as a natural flame said to burn continuously from fissures in the rock, fed by underground natural gas.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple's sanctum has no idol; nine small flames burning from the bare rock are worshipped as different aspects of the goddess. Mughal emperor Akbar is said to have tried, and failed, to extinguish the flame, later gifting a gold parasol to the shrine in acknowledgment.
