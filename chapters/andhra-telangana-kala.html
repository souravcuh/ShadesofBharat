आंध्र प्रदेश व तेलंगाना की कला

Art of Andhra Pradesh & Telangana

Hand-painted temple cloth and one of India's most technically precise ikat weaves both trace back to this shared Telugu-speaking cultural region.

राज्य अनुसार कला · State-wise Art

State & Region
सांस्कृतिक परिचय

Cultural Overview

Kalamkari's two distinct styles, Srikalahasti's freehand pen-work and Machilipatnam's block-printed cloth, developed side by side within a few hundred kilometers of each other, one for temple use, one for wider trade.

प्रमुख कला रूप

Notable Art Forms

Pochampally's ikat weaving, in Telangana, carries Geographical Indication recognition of its own alongside Odisha's and Gujarat's ikat traditions already covered on this site, and Nirmal's lacquered toy and painting craft adds a smaller but distinct regional tradition to the wider region's craft range.
