हरसिद्धि, उज्जैन

Harsiddhi, Ujjain

In the same city as the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, but a separate temple — the goddess here worshipped as Harsiddhi, identified in tradition with Mahakali.

मध्य प्रदेश · Madhya Pradesh  —  देवी (हरसिद्धि / महाकाली) · Devi (Harsiddhi / Mahakali)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Ujjain's Harsiddhi temple is counted among the Shakti Peethas, tradition holding that Sati's elbow fell here; the goddess is worshipped in a form associated with Mahakali, distinct from the Shiva of the nearby Mahakaleshwar temple.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple is known for its pair of tall, lamp-studded towers (deepmalas) flanking the entrance, lit in full on festival nights. It sits a short walk from Mahakaleshwar, making the two together one of Ujjain's central pilgrimage circuits.
