सिद्धिविनायक, मुंबई

Siddhivinayak, Mumbai

One of Mumbai's most visited temples — Ganesha worshipped here as Siddhivinayak, "the one who grants siddhi (accomplishment)".

महाराष्ट्र · Maharashtra  —  गणेश · Ganesha

Avatar Mandir
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The temple's deity is carved from a single black stone, with an unusual four-armed form and the goddesses Riddhi and Siddhi (prosperity and accomplishment) seated at his sides — a detail devotees hold gives the shrine its particular potency for granting wishes.

मंदिर

The Temple

Built in 1801 by a local farmer, the temple grew from a small village shrine into one of India's wealthiest and most visited, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors especially on Tuesdays and during the Ganesh Chaturthi festival.
