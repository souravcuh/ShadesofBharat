केदारनाथ

Kedarnath

Shiva at the head of the Mandakini valley, roughly 3,580 m up — one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, and among the most physically demanding sites to reach on foot.

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand  —  शिव · Shiva

Himalayan Char DhamJyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

The Mahabharata tradition holds that the Pandavas, seeking Shiva's forgiveness after the Kurukshetra war, pursued him here; Shiva evaded them by taking the form of a bull and diving into the ground, leaving behind the irregular, hump-shaped rock still worshipped as the linga today.

मंदिर

The Temple

The stone temple, attributed in tradition to Adi Shankaracharya (who is also said to have taken samadhi nearby), survived the devastating 2013 Uttarakhand floods largely intact, sheltered by a boulder that split the floodwaters around it. It is reachable only by a roughly 16–18 km trek or pony/helicopter ride from Gaurikund.
