काशी विश्वनाथ

Kashi Vishwanath

On the Ganga's western bank in Varanasi — the city held to be Shiva's own, one he is said never to leave.

वाराणसी, उत्तर प्रदेश · Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Kashi (Varanasi) is described across the Puranas as one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities and Shiva's personal abode, "Vishwanath" meaning "lord of the universe." Dying within the city is traditionally held to grant liberation directly, without further rebirth.

मंदिर

The Temple

The present temple was built in 1780 by Ahilyabai Holkar, queen of Indore, after the site had been destroyed and rebuilt across several earlier centuries; its gold-plated dome, added in the 19th century, is among Varanasi's most recognizable landmarks. A newly built corridor now connects the temple directly to the Ganga's ghats.
