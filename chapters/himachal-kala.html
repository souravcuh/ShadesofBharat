हिमाचल प्रदेश की कला

Art of Himachal Pradesh

A woolen cap that signals its wearer's home valley captures something of Himachal Pradesh's craft identity — small, valley-specific traditions rather than one single statewide style.

राज्य अनुसार कला · State-wise Art

State & Region
सांस्कृतिक परिचय

Cultural Overview

The Himachali topi, its color and trim varying district by district, reflects a wider pattern across the state's crafts: Kullu and Kinnauri handwoven wool shawls each carry their own recognizable weave and border pattern, distinct enough that a trained eye can place a shawl's valley of origin at a glance.

प्रमुख कला रूप

Notable Art Forms

Chamba's rumal embroidery, fine silk-thread work historically used to wrap gifts and depict mythological scenes, and the state's wood-carved temple architecture in its higher valleys round out a craft identity built from many small, valley-bound traditions rather than one dominant style.
