ज्ञान-विज्ञान

Gyan-Vigyan

Ancient Bharatiya vigyan organized by field — philosophy, yoga, Ayurveda, life science, astronomy, astrology, architecture, and literature — knowledge as a living science, each field anchored by the granthas, the texts, that carried it forward. Tap any card below for the fuller story.

दर्शन शास्त्र

Philosophy

Six classical schools of philosophy debated logic, matter, the self, and liberation for centuries — and the Gita and Shankara's commentaries remain the tradition's most-read philosophical texts today.

षड्दर्शन
Shad Darshana (Six Schools of Philosophy)
वैदिक व उत्तर-वैदिक काल · Vedic and post-Vedic era

Six classical schools of philosophy, each built on its own foundational sutras, disagreeing openly with one another about logic, matter, the self, and liberation — argument as a form of scholarship in its own right.

भगवद्गीता
Bhagavad Gita
महाभारत के भीतर · within the Mahabharata

Krishna's counsel to Arjuna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra — 700 verses embedded within the Mahabharata that stand today as the tradition's single most translated and commented-upon text.

शांकर भाष्य
Shankara Bhashya
८वीं शताब्दी · 8th century CE

Adi Shankaracharya's commentaries on the Upanishads, the Brahma Sutras, and the Bhagavad Gita — the texts that gave Advaita Vedanta its systematic philosophical form.

योग विज्ञान

Yoga Vigyan

Yoga as a systematic science of mind, not just a physical practice — Patanjali's framework for turning scattered older techniques into a single coherent path.

योग विज्ञान
Yoga Vigyan (The Yoga Sutras)
लगभग २री शताब्दी ई.पू. · circa 2nd century BCE

Patanjali's systematic 196-aphorism text turning yoga's scattered older practices into a single coherent path — the ashtanga, eight-limbed structure still taught in yoga instruction worldwide.

आयुर्वेद

Ayurveda

The tradition's clinical medicine — internal medicine and surgery, each with its own foundational text still referenced in Ayurvedic colleges today.

चरक संहिता
Charak Samhita
लगभग १–२री शताब्दी · circa 1st-2nd century CE

The foundational text of Ayurvedic internal medicine, compiled by the physician Charaka and still part of Ayurvedic education today.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
सुश्रुत संहिता
Sushruta Samhita
लगभग ६ठी शताब्दी ई.पू. · circa 6th century BCE

The Ayurvedic tradition's foundational surgical text, attributed to Sushruta, cataloguing over a hundred instruments and describing procedures including early reconstructive surgery.

आयुर्विज्ञान

Ayurvigyan — Life & Longevity Science

Alongside clinical treatment, a diagnostic and preventive science in its own right — reading the body's balance directly and working to extend healthy life rather than only cure illness.

आयुर्विज्ञान
Ayurvigyan (Life & Longevity Science)
आयुर्वेद परंपरा · Ayurvedic tradition

Ayurveda's diagnostic and preventive science beyond treatment alone — pulse reading, rejuvenation therapy, and a physiology built on the three doshas that still frames how Ayurvedic practitioners assess a person today.

खगोल विज्ञान

Astronomy

Working systems for tracking the sun, moon, and planets, calculating eclipses, and measuring the length of the year — centuries of observation refined into verse.

सूर्य सिद्धांत
Surya Siddhanta
वर्तमान रूप लगभग ४–५वीं शताब्दी · present form circa 4th-5th century CE

One of the oldest surviving texts of Indian astronomy, presented as knowledge revealed by the sun god Surya — a working system for calculating planetary positions and eclipses.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
आर्यभटीय
Aryabhatiya
४९९ ई. · 499 CE

Aryabhata's compact 5th-century text of astronomical and mathematical verses — the origin point for a value of pi, a rotating earth, and a systematic explanation of eclipses within the tradition.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
ज्योतिष

Jyotisha — Astrology

One of the six Vedangas — the discipline built on astronomy's calculations, but aimed at timing human affairs rather than only tracking the sky.

ज्योतिष शास्त्र
Jyotisha Shastra (Astrology)
वेदांग, वैदिक काल · Vedanga, Vedic era

One of the six Vedangas, limbs of Vedic study — the discipline of tracking time, planetary positions, and their bearing on human affairs, still consulted today for everything from wedding dates to naming ceremonies.

वास्तु व स्थापत्य

Vastu & Architecture

The science of aligning built space with cardinal direction and cosmic order — the reasoning behind temple orientation across this site's Tirtha chapter.

वास्तु शास्त्र
Vastu Shastra (Architecture)
लगभग १–६ठी शताब्दी · circa 1st-6th century CE

The traditional science of architecture and space, aligning homes, temples, and entire towns with cardinal direction and cosmic order — the reasoning behind why so many temples on this site's Tirtha chapter face a specific way.

साहित्य व नाट्य

Literature, Drama & the Arts

The foundational text behind Sanskrit drama, dance, and poetics — and the theory of rasa that still shapes how classical Indian performance is taught.

नाट्यशास्त्र
Natya Shastra (Literature, Drama & the Arts)
लगभग २री शताब्दी ई.पू. – २री शताब्दी · circa 2nd century BCE - 2nd century CE

Bharata Muni's foundational treatise on drama, dance, and music — the text that gave Sanskrit literature its theory of rasa, the aesthetic emotion a performance is meant to evoke in its audience.

वेद व पुराण

Vedas & Puranas

Behind every field above sits an older and larger textual inheritance — the Vedas as shruti, heard revelation, and the Puranas as smriti, remembered tradition. A fuller treatment of each is still to come in this site's Dharma & Shastra chapter; these two cards give the overview.

चार वेद
The Four Vedas
वैदिक काल · Vedic era

The oldest layer of the tradition's textual inheritance, Rigveda, Yajurveda, Samaveda, and Atharvaveda, traditionally held as apaurusheya, without human author, heard rather than composed.

विस्तार से पढ़ें · Read more →
अठारह पुराण
The Eighteen Puranas
पौराणिक काल · Puranic era

Eighteen major Puranas, traditionally compiled by Ved Vyasa, weaving cosmology, mythology, genealogy, and devotion into the stories most Hindus actually grow up hearing.

ग्रंथों के संरक्षक

Guardians of the Texts

Preservation is its own kind of knowledge-work. This is where the ancient granthas above meet a distinctly modern chapter in how they reached ordinary households.

गीता प्रेस
Gita Press
स्थापना १९२३ · founded 1923

A Gorakhpur publishing house founded in 1923 that put affordable, standardized copies of the Gita, Ramayana, and Puranas into millions of Indian homes across the 20th century.
