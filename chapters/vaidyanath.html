वैद्यनाथ

Vaidyanath

Shiva as "the physician lord" — worshipped here in an aspect associated with healing, at the center of the Deoghar temple town.

देवघर, झारखंड · Deoghar, Jharkhand  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition connects the site to Ravana, who is said to have performed severe penance here seeking to move Shiva's abode to Lanka; Shiva agreed on condition the linga never touch the ground en route, a condition Ravana was tricked into breaking near Deoghar, fixing the linga's location permanently.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple complex at Deoghar includes the main Vaidyanath shrine along with a cluster of smaller temples to related deities within the same walled courtyard. It draws large numbers of kanwariyas — pilgrims who carry Ganga water on foot from Sultanganj, roughly 100 km away, to offer here during the month of Shravan.
