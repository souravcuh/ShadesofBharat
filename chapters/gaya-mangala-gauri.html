मंगला गौरी, गया

Mangala Gauri, Gaya

Atop a hill in Gaya — a city better known for the nearby Vishnupad temple and for Bodh Gaya, here also home to one of the eighteen Maha Shakti Peethas.

बिहार · Bihar  —  देवी (मंगला गौरी) · Devi (Mangala Gauri)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Tradition holds this as the spot where Sati's breast fell, giving the goddess her name here, Mangala Gauri — "the auspicious fair one." Gaya itself is separately revered as a center for pind-daan, rites performed for one's ancestors, making the city a convergence point of several distinct traditions.

मंदिर

The Temple

The shrine sits on Mangalagauri hill, reached by a flight of stone steps, and is traditionally visited alongside the nearby Vishnupad temple as part of a wider Gaya pilgrimage that draws visitors for both ancestral rites and Devi worship.
