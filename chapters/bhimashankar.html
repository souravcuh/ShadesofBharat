भीमाशंकर

Bhimashankar

Set in the Sahyadri hills west of Pune, inside what is now a protected forest reserve and the source of the Bhima river.

महाराष्ट्र · Maharashtra  —  शिव · Shiva

Jyotirlinga
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Puranic tradition holds that Shiva took the fierce form of Bhima here to destroy the demon Tripurasura, and that the sweat from that battle gave rise to the Bhima river, which flows from near the temple.

मंदिर

The Temple

The temple sits inside the Bhimashankar Wildlife Sanctuary in the Sahyadri range, its forest setting distinct from most of the other eleven sites. The current structure dates largely from the 18th century, built in a blend of Nagara and later styles.
