शांतिनिकेतन शिल्प

Shantiniketan Crafts

A distinctive leather and batik craft tradition that grew directly out of Visva-Bharati University's art school, still sold from the same Shantiniketan workshops today.

शांतिनिकेतन, पश्चिम बंगाल · Shantiniketan, West Bengal

Handicraft
शिल्प व उद्गम

Craft & Origins

Best known for its engraved and dyed leatherwork, bags, sandals, and journals worked with incised floral and folk motifs, alongside batik-dyed cloth, the Shantiniketan craft tradition developed as part of Kala Bhavana, the art college Rabindranath Tagore founded within Visva-Bharati University, blending fine-art training with hands-on craft practice.

आज कहाँ जीवित है

Where It Lives Today

The weekly Sonajhuri haat (market) near Shantiniketan remains the craft's best-known outlet, where artisans sell leather goods, batik textiles, and Kantha-embroidered cloth directly, keeping the tradition tied to the same small town it grew from.
