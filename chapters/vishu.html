विशु

Vishu

The day opens with vishukkani — the first sight arranged the night before: rice, fruit, flowers, gold, and a mirror, believed to shape the fortune of the year ahead.

केरल

कथा एवं उत्पत्ति

Story & Origins

Some tellings tie the day to Krishna's slaying of the demon Narakasura — echoing the Naraka Chaturdashi story observed elsewhere in autumn, a rare case of the same legend anchoring festivals six months apart.

कैसे मनाया जाता है

How It's Kept

Elders gift children vishukkaineetam, small sums of money, after the vishukkani viewing at dawn. Firecrackers and a full family feast, sadya, round out the day.
