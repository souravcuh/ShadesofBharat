देवप्रयाग

Devprayag

The last and most sacred of the Panch Prayag — where the Alaknanda meets the Bhagirathi, and the river takes the name Ganga for the first time.

उत्तराखंड · Uttarakhand  —  अलकनंदा · भागीरथी · Alaknanda · Bhagirathi

SangamPanch Prayag
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Below Devprayag's confluence, the combined river is known as the Ganga proper — before this point, its two headwaters carry separate names and separate legends of their own descent to earth.

मंदिर

The Temple

The town sits dramatically at the point where the two rivers merge, distinguishable by color long before they fully blend. A temple to Raghunath (Rama) overlooks the confluence, and the site is traditionally treated as the ceremonial start of the Ganga's long course to the sea.
