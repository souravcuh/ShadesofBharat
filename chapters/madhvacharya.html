मध्वाचार्य

Madhvacharya

The philosopher-saint of Udupi who founded Dvaita Vedanta, arguing for an eternal, real distinction between the soul and the divine.

१३वीं शताब्दी · 13th century CE

AcharyaDvaita Vedanta
जीवन व कथा

Life & Story

Born near Udupi in coastal Karnataka, Madhva broke further from Advaita than Ramanuja had, holding that the individual soul (jiva) and the supreme (Vishnu) remain eternally and fundamentally distinct — a dualist philosophy setting him apart from both Shankara and Ramanuja.

योगदान व विरासत

Contribution & Legacy

He founded the Krishna Matha at Udupi, still active today, and his Dvaita school directly influenced the later Bhakti movement's devotional intensity, including its emphasis on a personal, ever-distinct relationship between devotee and god — a thread later carried forward by saints like Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.
