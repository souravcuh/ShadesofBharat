कामाक्षी अम्मन, कांचीपुरम

Kamakshi Amman, Kanchipuram

In one of South India's great temple cities — Kamakshi, "eyes of desire," worshipped here in a form tradition connects to Adi Shankaracharya's own teaching.

तमिलनाडु · Tamil Nadu  —  देवी (कामाक्षी) · Devi (Kamakshi)

Shakti Peetha
पौराणिक कथा

Legend & Significance

Kanchipuram is counted among the sapta-puri, the seven cities of liberation, and the Kamakshi temple stands among the eighteen Maha Shakti Peethas named in Adi Shankaracharya's hymn to the goddess across the subcontinent.

मंदिर

The Temple

Tradition holds that Adi Shankaracharya himself installed the Sri Chakra yantra before the goddess here and established formal worship rites still followed today. The temple sits among Kanchipuram's many other shrines, in a city long associated with both Shaiva and Vaishnava learning.
